The Experience

First Cafe I visited in Wattala, We were hungry and was randomly looking for a cafe. Then we found the perfect hideaway. I was really suprised when i first entered the cafe, never though it would be so nice and quiet

What suprised me the most is the range of foods and beverages. Once of the things I liked the most was the variety of beverage options available. The service is a must to mention that the staff was really friendly and helpful. They made sure we had a great experience and deliver the food within a reasonable time.

It's not only a dating spot. The vibe and the athmosphere is really cool to hangout with the friends. The music and the lighting create a relaxing and enjoyable environment. Overall, I had a great experience at Cafe Ivy and would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for a cozy and welcoming cafe in Wattala.

This Cafe enlighten me with the though "There are more hidden gems to explore!"
