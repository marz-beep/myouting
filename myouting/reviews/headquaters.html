The Experience

HQ by W15, I have heard about it so though to give it a visit. The place is so cool that gives you a unique interior. The posters, Street Lights and Rails is something different that really makes the place unique and vibing. Lovely locations in the resturant to have snaps and selfies.

I kicked off with a beer. It was chilled and perfect for the atmosphere. As for the dish I had a kottu and I really enjoyed it. Service was really good too.

Few things about the vibe, the DJ was really nice and when it blends with the rooftop view of the city, it was a perfect combination. The place is really good for a date or a night out with friends.
