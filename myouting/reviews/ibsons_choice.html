The Experience

We visited Ibson's Choice Cafe on a Night Time, Weekend. My first impression was it's too fancy, but when I enter the space, I got carried away with the decos and interiors. I was staring at the mini decos for minuites while my partner as doing the ordering. If you are a Anime, Marvel or DC fan, trust me! This place is worth a visit!

As for starters, the Bubble Tea and the Coffee were delicious. And then not to mention the board games while we having the beverages, was playing "Tak Tik Tuk". While i noticed that there were games as Uno, Jenga, Snake and Ladders etc..

Next stop is the food, it was so amazing. For me that's up to the taste that I have. I had a creamy paasta while my partner had a Pizza. The Pizza was so delicious that almost every chunk of bites had the cheese and the toppings.

Never Imagined there would be a DJ in the balcony, it was a surprise! (Went on a Saturday Night!)
